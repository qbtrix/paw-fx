Vol. III · Emergence

Murmuration

Five thousand wings, no conductor — only three small rules and a hawk in the dusk.

Each bird sees only neighbours within a small radius and obeys three Reynolds rules — separation (avoid crowding), alignment (match headings), cohesion (drift toward the local centre). Neighbours are found through a uniform spatial-hash grid, so the flock stays O(n) at thousands of birds. A predator (move the mouse over the canvas, or let it wander) triggers fleeing — the rolling flash-waves of a real starling murmuration emerge with no leader at all.
