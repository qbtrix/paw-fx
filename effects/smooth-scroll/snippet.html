How it works

Four steps, and the first one takes a minute.

Replace this copy with your own. Mount this section once anywhere on the page and the whole document scrolls on inertia instead of in jumps.

  1. Connect the source

    Point it at the repository, the warehouse or the bucket you already have. Nothing moves until you say so.

  2. Pick what matters

    Choose the tables, the folders or the branches worth watching. The rest stays where it is.

  3. Set the guardrails

    Decide who can approve, who gets paged and what never leaves the account. Written once, enforced everywhere.

  4. Ship it

    Open the dashboard on Monday and the week is already laid out. Change anything without filing a ticket.
