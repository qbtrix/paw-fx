Glossary

In the year of our Sync 2085, the air we breathe feels borrowed and the sunlight is a flux of LED warmth. We commune not through touch but through LinkLaces, every feeling filtered through the interface before it lands.

In the hush of the night some of us still hear the weeping of Gaia-Prime — a name kept by those who go on reading the forbidden archives.

Sync 2085 The standard protocol for holding a human consciousness in step with the network. Everything after it is measured from here. LinkLaces Two-way conduits carrying thought, feeling and sensation between people who never share a room. Gaia-Prime The unaltered state of the old ecosystem, kept now only in preservation zones and in very good simulations.
